# How Machines Learned to See

A two-minute documentary about computer vision, from MIT's 1966 Summer Vision
Project to the neural networks that now watch every frame. It has narration,
real footage and motion graphics.

- **Film:** [`output/how-machines-learned-to-see.mp4`](output/how-machines-learned-to-see.mp4)
  (1080p, 30 fps, about 2:01). English subtitles are included as a soft track.
- **Smaller copy:** [`output/how-machines-learned-to-see-720p.mp4`](output/how-machines-learned-to-see-720p.mp4) (720p, 26 MB)
- **Narration audio (voice only):** [`output/narration.mp3`](output/narration.mp3)
- **Subtitles:** [`output/how-machines-learned-to-see.srt`](output/how-machines-learned-to-see.srt)
- **Narration script and shot list:** [`NARRATION.md`](NARRATION.md)

## Second film: The Calmest Man on the Plane

A two-minute true-crime breakdown of the 1971 D.B. Cooper hijacking, made in the
style of psychological-analysis crime channels. It uses FBI archival material
(public domain), a photo of the actual aircraft, NASA b-roll, an animated route
map and a synthesized dark score.

- **Film:** [`output/the-calmest-man-on-the-plane.mp4`](output/the-calmest-man-on-the-plane.mp4) (1080p, 2:03, typewriter-style text), plus a [720p copy](output/the-calmest-man-on-the-plane-720p.mp4)
- **Czech version:** [`output/nejklidnejsi-muz-v-letadle.mp4`](output/nejklidnejsi-muz-v-letadle.mp4) (2:38), plus a [720p copy](output/nejklidnejsi-muz-v-letadle-720p.mp4). It has Czech narration and Czech on-screen text.
- **Script, sources, and what's archival vs. illustrative:** [`cooper/SCRIPT.md`](cooper/SCRIPT.md)
- **Rebuild both versions:** `cooper/make.sh`
- **Code:** `cooper/` (narration, timeline, scenes, render, audio, finalize). It reuses the helpers in `src/`.

## Third film: Stalin na splátky

A fast-paced, five-minute Czech documentary about Stalin's monument on Letná in
Prague. It covers the largest group statue in Europe, the sculptor who did not
live to see it unveiled, the 1962 demolition and the hollow plinth Prague still
pays for. The style follows "Jirka vysvětluje věci", with a Vox-style collage
layer on top: paper textures, cutouts, stamps, highlighter, hand-drawn marks,
maps and cross-sections.

- **Film:** `output/stalin-na-splatky.mp4` (1080p, 30 fps, about 5:15, Czech subtitles as a soft track), plus a 720p copy
- **Script** (written to the FacelessOS rules, greenlight-audited): [`stalin/SCRIPT.md`](stalin/SCRIPT.md); **sources:** [`stalin/SOURCES.md`](stalin/SOURCES.md); **credits:** [`stalin/CREDITS.md`](stalin/CREDITS.md)
- **Voice, sound effects and music:** ElevenLabs (eleven_v4, voice "Kuba"; sound generation; Eleven Music)
- **Rebuild:** `stalin/make.sh`

| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Script | `stalin/script.py` | Parses SCRIPT.md into narration paragraphs and visual/sound cues |
| Voice | `stalin/tts.py` | ElevenLabs narration with per-character timestamps |
| Timing | `stalin/layout.py` | Places the paragraphs, title and chapter cards; times every cue to its word |
| Plan | `stalin/plan.py` | One shot per cue (93 shots), with assets, animation and sound |
| Picture | `stalin/vox.py`, `shots.py`, `extras.py`, `maps.py`, `diagrams.py`, `render.py` | The collage toolkit, shot types and the parallel renderer |
| Material | `stalin/fetch.py`, `stalin/prep.py` | Google Images (Serper), Pexels and yt-dlp downloads, contact sheets, background removal |
| Sound | `stalin/sfx.py`, `stalin/music.py`, `stalin/audio.py` | Effect library, five music cues, and a mix that ducks the music under the voice |
| Final | `stalin/finalize.py` | Muxes, normalizes loudness to -14 LUFS, writes subtitles and the 720p copy |

## How it's made

Everything is generated by the code in `src/`; nothing was edited by hand.

| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Script | `src/narration.py` | The narration, split into sentences |
| Voice | `src/tts.py` | Neural text-to-speech, run locally (Kokoro). Records when each sentence starts so the picture can cut to it |
| Detection | `src/detect.py` | Runs YOLOX-S (objects) and YuNet (faces) on the real footage and caches every box |
| Examples | `src/crops.py` | Cuts labelled example images out of the footage using those detections |
| Timeline | `src/timeline.py` | Lays the ten scenes out against the length of the voice |
| Picture | `src/scenes_*.py`, `src/fx.py`, `src/render.py` | Draws each scene frame by frame (footage, HUD, charts, animations) and renders the scenes in parallel |
| Sound | `src/audio.py` | Synthesizes an original score and sound effects in numpy, timed to on-screen events, and ducks the music under the voice |
| Final | `src/finalize.py` | Joins the scenes, normalizes loudness to -16 LUFS, and writes the MP4 and SRT |

To rebuild from scratch, run `./make.sh` (Python 3, ffmpeg and network access
to GitHub). It takes about 20–30 minutes on 4 cores.
