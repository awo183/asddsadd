- **Narration:** [Kokoro-82M](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx) neural text-to-speech (Apache-2.0), voice *am_michael*.
- **Music and sound effects:** original, synthesised in `pipeline/audio.py`.
- **Maps:** [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain).
- **Typefaces (SIL OFL / Apache-2.0, via Google Fonts):** Big Shoulders Stencil Display, Big Shoulders Display, Source Serif 4, Special Elite, Courier Prime, Playfair Display.
- **Motion graphics:** drawn in `pipeline/anims.py`.
