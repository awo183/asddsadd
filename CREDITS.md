- Narration: synthesized offline with Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), voices af_heart (narrator), bm_george (Hardy), am_michael (Ramanujan).
- Music: original procedural score generated by `documentary/music.py` (no third-party audio).
- Maps: Natural Earth (public domain).
- Fonts: Cormorant Garamond, EB Garamond, Spectral, Inter, Caveat (SIL OFL); Homemade Apple, Special Elite (Apache 2.0).
- Mathematics rendered with matplotlib mathtext; animations drawn with skia-python.
